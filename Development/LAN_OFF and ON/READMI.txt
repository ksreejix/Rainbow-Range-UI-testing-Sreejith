Run this in Administrator PowerShell from C:\LAN:

powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\toggle-ethernet.ps1

The script cycles the Ethernet adapter: connected for 60 seconds, then disconnected for 10 seconds. Press Ctrl+C to stop; it will attempt to re-enable Ethernet before exiting.

--> powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\toggle-ethernet.ps1
